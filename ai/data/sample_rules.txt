Attendance Rules

A student must have at least 75 percent attendance in each subject to be eligible for the end semester exam.
Students with attendance between 65 and 75 percent can apply for condonation by paying a fee of 500 rupees.
Students with attendance below 65 percent are detained and must repeat the semester.

Examination Rules

Each theory subject has 40 marks for internal assessment and 60 marks for the end semester exam.
A student must score at least 40 percent overall to pass a subject.
Students who fail can appear for the supplementary exam held after the results are declared.

Hostel Rules

Hostel fee must be paid before the 10th of every semester.
Students must return to the hostel by 9 PM on weekdays.
Visitors are allowed only in the common room between 4 PM and 6 PM.
